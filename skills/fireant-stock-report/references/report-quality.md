# Report quality contract

The report is a compact research memo, not a dense market terminal.

Required hierarchy:

1. Source/date masthead.
2. Symbol title and rules-based assessment.
3. Three-item plain-language summary.
4. Six primary market metrics.
5. Price chart, annual table and fundamental trends.
6. Score components and news topics.
7. Initially collapsed, filterable news stream.
8. Methodology, disclaimer and source.

Use a neutral paper/panel/ink palette with `#1f6f4a` as the primary accent. Reserve the muted red for negative values. Avoid gradients, decorative colors, oversized dashboards and excessive cards.

On presentation changes, generate a fresh report and inspect the actual browser render. Test all sentiment filters and progressive news expansion. Do not claim visual quality from source inspection alone.
